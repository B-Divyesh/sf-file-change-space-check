Terms for using the estimate

Use the plan before a copy, and keep backups of important files.

License

The software is free and open source under the MIT License. You may use, inspect, modify, and redistribute it under that license.

What the estimate covers

The CLI reports a metadata-based plan and conservative space bounds. A filesystem or copy tool may allocate data differently.

Readable metadata does not prove that a later copy can write. The CLI does not execute its manifest.

Your responsibility

Review the paths, conflict policy, verdict, and manifest before starting another copy tool. Keep a tested backup of important data.
